
### Grading

The course will be graded based on the three assignments:  
**Assignment 1**: 35%; individual; due Week 5  
**Assignment 2**: 35%; group; due Week 10  
**Assignment 3**: 30%; individual & group; due Week 14

The grade for each assignment must be at least a 5. The final grade must be at least 5.5. Group sizes may vary depending on the number of students enrolled.

There will be **no resits** for assignments. Late submissions will be accepted only for valid reasons (e.g., health issues).
